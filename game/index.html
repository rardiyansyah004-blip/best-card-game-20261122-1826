How to play

  • Both duelists begin with 20 life. First to 0 loses.
  • Each turn your mana grows by 1 (up to 10). Cards cost mana to play.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same suit in one turn and the second gains +2 power.
  • Watch the Duchess’s intent — she announces what she will do, so you can shield or strike first.
  • Draw a card at the start of every turn. Empty your rival’s life to win.
